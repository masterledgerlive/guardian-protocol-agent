Proven Player

AV1 chunks stay locked until the 448-byte receipt matches. This player does not share a buffer with any other player.

Encode

SVT-AV1 1.7.0 on CPU compresses each ingest. The demo chunks are one second so the leader sequence is easy to see. The protocol target is five seconds.

Bind

SHA-256 binds the source digest, the AV1 bytes, and a slice Merkle root. The Groth16 slot stays zero. A stuffed slot will not unlock.

Decode

After the receipt checks, the MP4 is handed to the video element. Chromium and Firefox decode AV1 with dav1d.
