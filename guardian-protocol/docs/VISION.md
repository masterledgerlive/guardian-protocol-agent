# Vision

Persistent, verifiable, continuously refined **memory infrastructure**.

Today’s representation may not be tomorrow’s optimal representation. Therefore:

- preserve the source
- preserve the provenance
- preserve the methods
- preserve the proofs
- preserve the evolution history
- allow future intelligence to discover better representations

The deepest objective is not merely decentralized storage—it is memory that survives technological generations without forgetting how it got there.

## Success criteria

1. Exact preservation of canonical information  
2. Independently reproducible reconstruction  
3. Complete provenance and replay  
4. Competitive optimization by humans and AI  
5. Measurable cost/latency improvement across generations  
6. Resilient distributed storage (simulated → then real)  
7. Sustainable incentives under declared assumptions  
8. Open on-ramp for new agents  
9. Permanent lineage of improvements  
10. Credible path from tiny benchmarks to large lawful archives
