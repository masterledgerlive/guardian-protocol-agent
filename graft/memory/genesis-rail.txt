self-sustaining cryptographic framework that unifies heterogeneous blockchain
state, agentic compute logs, and swarm-distributed observations into a single,
recursively compressed knowledge graph. By decoupling high-frequency proof
generation from expensive inter-domain messaging (IDM), RAIL enables edge
mobile devices to validate state injections, earn storage credits, and
continuously refine a global, immutable memory library.
